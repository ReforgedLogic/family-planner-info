Add your calendar to our family planner

Our kitchen planner shows the calendars people share with it, each in its own color. It only reads your events. It never changes them, and you can stop sharing any time.

Google Calendar (Android or iPhone)

  1. On your phone, open Chrome (or Safari) and go to calendar.google.com. If it opens the Calendar app or a simple page, use the browser menu and turn on Desktop site.
  2. Tap the ⚙ Settings gear at the top right, then Settings.
  3. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, tap your name.
  4. Find Share with specific people or groups and tap + Add people and groups.
  5. Enter the planner's email:
    carney.planner@gmail.com
  6. Set permission to See all event details and tap Send.
  7. Tell Jerard or Kirstin the email address you use for Google Calendar. They'll add you on the planner in a few seconds.

Want them to see only when you're busy? Pick See only free/busy instead.

iPhone calendar (iCloud)

  1. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the ⓘ next to the calendar you want to share.
  3. Turn on Public Calendar, then tap Share Link…
  4. Text the link to Jerard or Kirstin. They'll paste it into the planner.

A public calendar link lets anyone who has the link see that calendar, so only send it to us.

Outlook

  1. In Outlook on the web, open Settings, then Calendar, then Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, pick your calendar and Can view all details, then Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link and text it to Jerard or Kirstin.
