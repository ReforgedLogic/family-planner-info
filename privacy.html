Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Family Planner is a private app used by one family on its own home server. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

With the family's permission, Family Planner reads the Google Calendars shared with the family's planner account so it can show them on the kitchen screen, and it adds, changes or removes events the family creates from the planner.

Where the data goes

Calendar data is stored only on the family's own server at home. It is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or sent anywhere other than back to Google Calendar when the family adds or changes an event. When the family uses the optional assistant feature, the request and the calendar details it needs are sent to the AI service that answers it.

Google API Services

Family Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps and services), and the planner's stored data can be deleted from the home server.
